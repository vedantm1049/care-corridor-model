      Where the price should sit

      Every case is priced on its own. A quote depends on the patient's clinical risk, the hospital and its current contract price, the add-ons the patient actually uses, and the insurer's plan and paid rate. Nothing here is a flat fee or a rate card. Use the floor and the ceiling as guardrails for each quote: never below the floor, never above the ceiling.

      The floor is what a case costs the provider to deliver. The ceiling is the most an insurer will pay and still keep a saving worth having. Any all-in price between the two works for both sides. Every input below is a placeholder until you enter your own numbers.

      Why members would choose India

      The saving only happens if members opt in. These are the reasons they would, and the cases where they should stay on the UAE route.

      Questions members will ask

      What if something goes wrong?The hospital stay is extended as needed, and the all-in price includes a reserve for it. The UAE doctor stays in touch throughout.
      Will I lose more days off work?No. Recovery takes the same time wherever the surgery happens. The member simply recovers in India first.
      Who handles visas, flights and transfers?The provider arranges flights, any visas needed (Indian passport holders need none), and transfers where the package includes them.
      What happens after I come home?Physio and nursing continue at home in the UAE, and the doctor who saw you before the trip follows up.

      Who should stay on the UAE route

      • Emergencies and anything that cannot wait for travel.
      • Members the UAE doctor judges unfit to fly, or too complex to treat away from home.
      • Treatment that needs long follow-up, such as chemotherapy.
      • Anyone who prefers not to travel. Opting out carries no penalty.

      Patient journey

        Why one UAE partner

        Coordinators such as Vaidam and Lyfboat hand the member over at the airport. Here the same UAE clinical team is responsible from the first consult to the 90-day outcome.

        Clinical oversight at both ends

        A UAE doctor reviews the case before travel, follows recovery by video, and delivers home care after return.

        Accredited hospitals only

        Partner hospitals are vetted for accreditation, surgical volume and an international patient desk.

        Outcomes you can audit

        Complications, readmissions and member scores are reported for every case at 90 days.

        What you get

        • Members opt in. No one is asked to travel against their wishes.
        • One all-in price per case, agreed before travel.
        • Nothing paid up front by the member.
        • Rehab continues at home in the UAE after return.
        • Managed care fee is a share of the saving, so it applies only when you save.

        Why a UAE provider, not a facilitator

        Coordinators such as Vaidam, Lyfboat and My 1Health already sell trips to India. They hand the patient over at the airport. A UAE provider can own both ends.

        It already holds the payer

        Insurer and employer contracts exist today. A coordinator has to win them one at a time.

        It owns the patient at home

        Teleconsults before the trip and home nursing after it are the provider's own services, billed in the UAE.

        It has a foot in India

        An Indian clinical team, in-house or through a partner, vets partner hospitals and runs the recovery network.

          Method

          • UAE side is the surgery package price in Dubai from public 2026 price guides and hospital packages. Insurer-negotiated rates can be lower, so the slider lets you enter the real figure.
          • India side uses international-patient package ranges. Most published ranges come from metro hospitals, so tier-2 quotes should land at or below them, and substantially below once prices are negotiated for bulk volume.
          • Like for like. Rehab after discharge is counted on both sides at the same visit prices. In the corridor, most physio happens in India during recovery and the rest at home through the provider. Most of the India stay is in an apartment, not a hospital.
          • Retail on both sides. UAE prices are package or guide prices, not insurer rates. India prices are hospital list prices, for foreigners or for Indian patients.
          • Pricing is case by case. The case estimator shows a share of the saving (20%, minimum AED 3,000) or a fee quoted for the case. The Pricing tab sets the floor and ceiling for each quote and compares three ways to quote a case. None of it is a rate card.
          • Add-ons are optional. Companion, apartment, nurse, meals, transfers, legroom seats, visas, physio in India, teleconsults and the digital programme can each be switched off. Rehab after discharge switches off on both sides together.
          • Digital programmes. A 90-day app-based recovery programme is added for heart cases only. Physio in India is a provider-vetted network of specialist physiotherapists.
          • Managed care. Coordination, teleconsults, specialist physio in India, home visits after return and a 90-day digital recovery programme for heart cases, shown as one line.
          • Estimates, clearly marked. Apartment, transfers, legroom seats, meals, visa and non-Kochi airfares are estimates. Provider fee and visit prices are placeholders.
          • Defaults are the leanest case. Value hospital, Indian-patient price, and bare minimum add-ons: hospital, the patient's flight, teleconsults, physio and rehab. Switch on what each patient needs.
          • Angioplasty replaces sleeve gastrectomy, which did not save money. Planned cases only, priced for one stent. Emergency cases after a heart attack stay on the UAE route.
          • Not covered yet: Saudi baselines, organ and bone marrow transplants, and cancer care beyond prostatectomy. Transplants need extra approvals in India for foreign patients.
          • Not medical advice. Fit-to-fly timings quote public guidance. The operating surgeon decides.
